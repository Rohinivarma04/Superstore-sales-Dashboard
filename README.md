# SuperStore Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive SuperStore Sales Dashboard developed
to analyze sales performance across different segments, regions,
categories, months, subcategories, and shipping modes.

The dashboard provides visual insights into sales trends and helps
compare business performance across different dimensions.

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales per Segment

The dashboard analyzes sales across different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 2. Sales per Region

Sales are analyzed across four regions:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 3. Sales by Month

The monthly sales chart shows sales trends from January to December,
helping identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### 4. Subcategory vs Revenue

The dashboard compares revenue across different product subcategories,
including:

- Tables
- Supplies
- Storage
- Phones
- Paper
- Machines
- Labels
- Furnishings
- Fasteners
- Envelopes
- Copiers
- Chairs
- Bookcases
- Binders
- Art
- Appliances
- Accessories

### 5. Sales by Category

Sales are compared across the major product categories:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### 6. Sales per Ship Mode

The dashboard analyzes sales across different shipping modes:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- Same Day
- First Class

## Dashboard Filters

Interactive filters are available for:

- Years
- Quarter

These filters allow users to explore sales performance for
different periods.

## Key Insights

- Consumer is one of the major customer segments shown in the dashboard.
- Sales performance can be compared across four regions.
- Monthly sales trends can be analyzed from January to December.
- Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies can be compared by category.
- Revenue can be compared across different product subcategories.
- Shipping modes can be analyzed based on sales performance.
